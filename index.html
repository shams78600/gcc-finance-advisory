Questions

Answers before you ask

Isn't this just an Excel job? Why not hire a cheaper freelancer?

A freelancer gives you a spreadsheet. I give you a diagnosis, a sized opportunity and a board-ready story, in three documents that tie to your TB down to 0.00 SAR. The Excel file is how I get there. The decision is what you're buying. Most clients find the cost of one undiagnosed loss driver is many times the fee.

How is my data kept confidential?

A mutual NDA can be signed before you share anything. Files are kept only for the engagement and deleted on request. If you're paying personally, nobody at your company ever needs to know I was involved. The work is delivered under your name.

What do I need to send?

Usually one file to start: a trial balance export, a sales/ERP extract, or your last financial statements. If anything is missing, I send one consolidated missing-data checklist instead of chasing you with questions.

Will the deliverables match our company format and IFRS?

Yes. Reports and decks use your branding and terminology. Statements follow IFRS presentation, including common reclassifications (e.g. freight from COGS to Selling & Distribution), with every adjustment documented.

Do you work with consulting firms on a white-label basis?

Yes. Deliverables go out under your firm's brand, with a strict NDA and no contact with your end client. Many partners use the monthly retainer as reserved analyst capacity.

Can my company pay by PO and invoice?

Yes. I provide a formal quotation, accept Purchase Orders, and issue a tax invoice with TRN. Individuals can pay by card, bank transfer or STC Pay.

What if the board or auditor challenges a number?

That's what the tie-out guarantee is for. Every figure traces back to source. If something doesn't tie, I fix it free. The walkthrough call and support period cover follow-up questions too.

Do you work outside Saudi Arabia?

Yes, across the GCC (UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman). Models use local parameters where relevant, and all pricing is in SAR for simplicity.
